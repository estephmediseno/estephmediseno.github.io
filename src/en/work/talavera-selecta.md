<section class="wrap prose">
  <h2>The problem</h2>
  <p>A factory is not a lifestyle club. If the content only seduces the household, the wholesaler has nowhere to stand. If the website only talks pallets, the household feels nothing.</p>

  <h2>The system</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Social for product, recipe, and origin — packaging on camera, not generic stock.</li>
    <li>WhatsApp as the closing channel, not an afterthought.</li>
    <li>A site I built: categories, plant, distributor pitch, recipes.</li>
    <li>The same person who decides how a cheese looks in Stories decides how wholesale is requested.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
