<section class="wrap prose">
  <h2>Concept and strategy</h2>
  <p>Gea embodies primordial origin, the earth, and the human silhouette in its natural form. The design deliberately departs from conventional lingerie advertising tropes: it avoids performing for an external gaze. The mark behaves like a continuous organic curve that contours the skin without rigid borders or gratuitous ornamentation.</p>

  <h2>Wordmark construction</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Modular grid:</strong> 12.3 × 7.6 X proportion.</li>
    <li><strong>Clear space:</strong> Defined by the full height of the lowercase letter <em>e</em> around the perimeter.</li>
    <li><strong>Usage rules:</strong> No disproportionate scaling, arbitrary color changes, stroke manipulation, or character rearrangement.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Color system</h2>
  <p>The palette pairs a restrained primary base with a secondary spectrum inspired by diverse skin tones:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Primary palette:</strong> Cool Grey (<code>#E5E5E5</code>), Neutral Black (<code>#000000</code> / rgb 29, 29, 27), and Pure White.</li>
    <li><strong>Secondary palette (skin tones):</strong>
      <ul>
        <li>Ébano (<code>#623B17</code>)</li>
        <li>Miel (<code>#C48C63</code>)</li>
        <li>Arena (<code>#E5BE8D</code>)</li>
        <li>Melocotón (<code>#EBD9A6</code>)</li>
        <li>Lirio pálido (<code>#EDD5BC</code>)</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Typography</h2>
  <p>The custom typographic system for Gea balances editorial elegance with technical readability:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Headlines:</strong> <em>Lovelace</em> — a classical, high-contrast serif with anatomical contours.</li>
    <li><strong>Body and text:</strong> <em>Poppins</em> — geometric, unadorned, and legible across packaging and product cards.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Photographic direction</h2>
  <p>The manual establishes an uncompromising photographic standard: essential imagery, strict black and white, zero cosmetic skin retouching or digital alteration, celebrating bodily diversity without studio artificiality.</p>

  <h2>Brand applications</h2>
  <p>The system extends from tactile packaging (matte black box with magnetic closure and the interior lid motto <em>“Viste tu piel con amor”</em>) to large-scale transit posters and digital media without dilution.</p>
</section>
